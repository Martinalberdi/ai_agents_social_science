# House style

- Plain English. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Every number says what, where, when, which group and which unit:
  "In September 2026, 22.8% of people under 25 in Spain's labour force were
  unemployed [EU1]."
- Differences between two percentages are in percentage points (pp).
- Never write "X% of young people are unemployed" for the unemployment rate.
  The rate is a share of the labour force. The share of all young people is
  the ratio [EU3].
- No causes unless a cited source says so. Avoid: because, due to, driven
  by, caused, led to, as a result of.
- Missing data: say which month is the latest available.
- Flags: if a value has a flag (e.g. "d", definition differs), say so.
- No recommendations unless `docs/commission.md` asks for them.
- Every sentence with a number ends with its source key, e.g. [EU1].
