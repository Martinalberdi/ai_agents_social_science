# Habit 1: who made the numbers?

For each source the agent proposes or adds:

1. Open the source. Do not rely on a summary of it.
2. Write down:
   - who made the numbers (organisation, and the survey or register);
   - original or copy: if it copies another source, find the original;
   - how the numbers were made, in one sentence;
   - who is missing (countries, ages, years, groups);
   - the terms of use.
3. Add this as one row to the project's source list (`docs/sources.csv` or
   `docs/sources.md`). Create the file if it does not exist.
4. Tell the user in two lines: who made the numbers, and one warning.
